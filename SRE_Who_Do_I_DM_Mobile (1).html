⏱ Response Time Expectations
  • Ops, TC, Marketing, Zillow Success Manager, and Mentors respond within business hours
  • Slack is not an emergency channel
  • For true emergencies: call or text the designated department leader directly
Urgent items should clearly state: what is urgent, why it meets urgency criteria, and what deadline is at risk.
❌ Common Misroutes
  • DM Leadership for day-to-day questions
  • DM TC before paperwork is submitted
  • DM Ops for deal strategy
  • DM Mentors for system access
  • DM TC for deal strategy or negotiation advice
  • DM Marketing Assistant for event requests
  • DM multiple people at once hoping for faster answers
This slows everyone down and breaks trust.
✅ What This Protects
  • Faster answers
  • Cleaner communication
  • Respect for everyone's role
  • Less friction between agents and staff
  • Clear accountability
This routing model is not optional. It is how SRE operates.
